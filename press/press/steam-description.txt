League of Rockets is a fast, physics-driven vehicular sports game built in the spirit of Rocket League. Pick from a roster of battle cars, drop into regulation arenas, and fight for the ball with boost, aerials, and precision drives. Play instantly in your browser — no download required.

## About This Game

### WHAT IS LEAGUE OF ROCKETS?
League of Rockets is a free-to-play, browser-based vehicular sports game that captures the adrenaline of Rocket League–style gameplay. You pilot a battle car across a regulation pitch, using boost, air rolls, and aerial precision to smash a glowing ball into the opponent's goal.

Built with Three.js for 3D rendering and Cannon.js for real-time physics, the game runs instantly in any modern browser with no install, no launcher, and no friction.

### CORE GAMEPLAY
- **Drive & steer** with WASD or arrow keys
- **Rocket boost** (Space / Shift) for supersonic aerials and speed bursts
- **Jump & double jump** to launch off the ground and walls
- **Air roll & pitch** while airborne to line up impossible shots
- **Ball cam / car cam** toggle with C
- **Powerslide & handbrake** (Shift) for tight cornering
- **Full physics** — collisions, ball bounces, and car contact feel real

### CARS
League of Rockets ships with an authentic 1:1 Octane battle car modeled in Blender, matching the look and proportions of the Rocket League Octane. Additional car variants — Dominus, Fennec, and Breakout — are in active development, each with a distinct stance, silhouette, and racing character.

### ARENAS
- **Sunset Championship** — an open-air hillside stadium with neon hex goals, curved ramp walls, and dramatic dusk lighting
- **Forbidden Temple** — a dense, atmospheric temple arena with layered geometry and dramatic sightlines
- More arenas in development, built in Blender and ported to the web

### PHYSICS & CONTROLS
The game uses Cannon.js for rigid-body physics and Three.js for rendering. Every car is a single rigid body with friction, restitution, and boost forces tuned for accessible but skill-expressive gameplay. The ball responds to car impact with realistic deflection and spin.

### WHO IT'S FOR
- Rocket League fans who want a fast, free, no-install alternative
- Players who prefer browser games and hate large downloads
- Anyone who likes vehicular combat, aerial mechanics, and physics-driven sports

### FREE & OPEN
League of Rockets is free to play. The source code and Blender assets are open on GitHub — contributions, car variants, and arena models are welcome.

## Features (Steam feature bullets)
- Free, browser-based vehicular sports game — no download, no install
- Authentic 1:1 Octane battle car, modeled in Blender
- Full real-time physics with Cannon.js — driving, aerials, collisions, ball bounce
- Rocket boost, jump, air roll, powerslide, and ball cam
- Sunset Championship and Forbidden Temple arenas, with more in development
- WASD / arrow key controls, designed for keyboard and controller
- Open-source project — car variants and arenas contributed by the community

## Technical
- Engine: Three.js (3D rendering) + Cannon.js (physics)
- Assets: Blender 5.x models (FBX / GLB pipeline)
- Platform: Web (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Input: Keyboard (WASD + Space/Shift) and gamepad
